harmony_bidding.agent_api.Agent inherits the upstream standard-library-only
simul_bidding_env.strategy.base_bidding_strategy.BaseBiddingStrategy abstraction.
Its CPA constructor and request-level signatures are not used. Local auction,
budget, PID, observations, dataset adapters, scoring and lifecycle are rewritten
for the user-supplied Harmony rules. No upstream model weights are used.
The new trajectory bridge can feed the upstream ReplayBuffer push interface.

This is a local development environment, not the organizer's official evaluator,
PID, data format, scoring implementation or acceptance certification.
